# Sources and Open Items (`automotive-cpp`)

> All external claims used to build `automotive-cpp` come from secondary public documentation and research summaries. Paywalled standards (MISRA C++:2023, ISO 26262-6:2018) and inaccessible documents were NOT read directly. Every external claim carries a `verify` label until a human confirms it against the primary text.

## 1. Sources (with verify labels)

- MISRA C++:2023 targets C++17 and merges MISRA C++:2008 with AUTOSAR C++14; guideline structure (rules vs directives), categories (Mandatory, Required with formal deviation, Advisory), decidable vs undecidable split, and analysis scopes (`single-unit-rules`, `system-decidable-rules`):
  - `[src: https://ch.mathworks.com/help/bugfinder/misra-cpp-2023-rules-and-directives.html — verify]`
  - `[src: https://www.mathworks.com/help/bugfinder/ref/checkmisrac2023misracpp2023.html — verify]`
  - `[src: https://www.parasoft.com/?p=8769 — verify]`

  - Note: guideline counts differ across vendor pages (179 vs 180 entries; 155 vs 156 decidable rules); confirm against official MISRA C++:2023.

  - `[src: https://www.qt.io/software-insights/misra-c2023-guidelines-the-intricacies-of-undecidability — verify]`
